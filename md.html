Managing Director

Chandula Perera

Trust plays a pivotal role in fostering enduring relationships, and this principle is especially pertinent in the context of the Sri Lankan printing industry. With a history that spans centuries, the printing sector was once a cornerstone of the nation's industrial landscape. However, the industry is now grappling with global challenges fueled by heightened consumer demands. In response to these challenges,

the JDC Group of Companies, founded in 1979 as a subsidiary of Nawaloka Holdings, has emerged as a prominent supplier of printing and packaging machinery and materials in Sri Lanka. Committed to quality, customer satisfaction, and the development of small-scale entrepreneurs within the printing industry, JDC stands as a testament to the power of trust in forging lasting client relationships and driving mutual growth.


The JDC Group of Companies, under the umbrella of Nawaloka Holdings and Redline Capital, embodies a customer-centric approach with a global perspective. The company's sustained success is rooted in its unwavering dedication to quality and customer-focused systems.

Director’s Message

Givanthi Dharmadasa

For the past 45 years, the JDC Group of Companies has been a stalwart in the Sri Lankan printing industry, marked by consistent growth, long-term success, and resilience in the face of challenges. Our unwavering mission revolves around delivering the highest quality products and services to our valued customers.

Our corporate vision centers on continuous improvement, with an unyielding commitment to customer satisfaction at its core. We have cultivated a culture of ethics and quality, resulting in a reputation for trustworthiness in Sri Lanka's printing sector.


Our dedicated management team upholds our core values, and we expect the same level of commitment from our employees and business partners. Integrity, honesty, competence, collaboration, creativity, and respect are the pillars that have underpinned JDC since its inception. Our vision is to provide an unrivaled customer experience.

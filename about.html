ABOUT JDC PRINTING TECHNOLOGIES (PVT) LTD

How We Grow

Established in 1979, JDC Printing Technologies (Pvt) Ltd. has become a leading supplier of cutting-edge machinery and materials to Sri Lanka's printing and packaging industry. Our comprehensive range of products and services empowers businesses to enhance productivity, reduce costs, and expand their operations. We import high-quality machinery and materials, offer specialized services, and have a rich history of pioneering innovations in the industry.


JDC Printing Technologies is dedicated to innovation and excellence. As industry pioneers, we introduced pre-press services for flexible packaging, Computer to Plate imaging for flexo plates, and offset CTP technology. Our commitment to staying at the forefront of technology enables us to meet the evolving needs of our valued customers while maintaining exceptional quality and service standards.

VISION - To be the best, reliable and innovative solution provider to the printing and packaging industry.

MISSION - JDC’s mission is to be the foremost and highly reliable service provider. With qualified and experienced staff in their respective applications, we offer value proposition and provide innovative ideas that helps our customers to grow.

FUTURE - JDC has embarked heavily on digital printing and has taken timely action to increase the business by offering solutions for digital to both our existing and new customers.

Company History

45+ years of experience in the
flexo & offset print industry

Leading supplier of machinery and materials to the printing ​and packaging industry in Sri Lanka from Prepress to Finishing.

We always make it a point to work very closely with all our overseas suppliers so that we carry out our marketing and support activities effectively. JDC has many pioneering achievements. We were the first to offer Prepress services for the flexible packaging industry followed by the installation of a CTP to image flexo and the offset plates in the Year 2002.

Our sister company JDC Graphic Systems is considered as one of the finest Prepress facilities in the industry, fully equipped with advanced prepress software systems & machinery from ESKO and Flint Germany.​We are a subsidiary of Nawaloka Holdings, ​a diversified conglomerate in Sri Lanka into Healthcare, Aviation, Engineering, Construction, Trading and Manufacturing​.

JDC Printing Technologies, established in 1979, is a leading provider of advanced machinery and materials to Sri Lanka's printing and packaging industry. Their extensive product range enhances customer productivity, reduces costs, and supports business expansion.

JDC offers diverse machinery and customizable solutions for packaging, labels, signs, and more, backed by application support and consultancy services.
